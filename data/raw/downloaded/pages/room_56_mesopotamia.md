# Mesopotamia, 6000-1500 BC

Mesopotamia witnessed crucial advancements in the development of human civilisation between 6000 and 1550 BC. The region lies between the Tigris and Euphrates rivers, covering modern Iraq, north-east Syria and part of south-east Turkey.

Objects displayed in Room 56 illustrate agricultural development, the invention of writing, advances in technology and artistry, and the achievements of Sumerians, Akkadians and Babylonians. Finds from the Royal Cemetery at Ur are especially important, including jewellery, pottery, musical instruments, tomb material and human remains.

Highlight: the Standard of Ur.
