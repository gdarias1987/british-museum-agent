# Mesopotamia, 1500-539 BC

Room 55 traces Babylonia under the Kassites and the growth of the Babylonian state and empire until the Persian conquest under Cyrus in 539 BC. It also covers the development and fall of the Assyrian state and empire.

Objects include boundary stones recording royal land grants, palace finds and clay tablets from Ashurbanipal's library at Nineveh. These tablets document Mesopotamian literature, scholarship and learning in cuneiform.

Highlight: the Babylonian Map of the World.
