# Tablet K.3375 - Epic of Gilgamesh

Clay tablet associated with the Epic of Gilgamesh and Ashurbanipal's library. It is displayed in Room 55, case 16. The British Museum record connects it with Mesopotamian myth and legend and cites major scholarly editions, including Andrew George's critical edition of the Babylonian Gilgamesh epic.
