# British Museum - Egypt and Middle East visitor context

The initial visitor corpus focuses on Egypt and the Middle East because these areas provide a strong, coherent route for a museum assistant demo.

Ground floor highlights include Room 4 and the Assyrian galleries in Rooms 6-10. Upper floor areas include Egyptian rooms 61-66 and Middle East rooms 42-43 and 52-59.

The assistant should treat room, floor and accessibility hints as first-class retrieval metadata. Visitor answers must cite source documents or explain when evidence is missing.

QR codes must not be assumed as a universal object-identification mechanism across the museum. The system should prefer room numbers, object labels, audio guide numbers and textual map alternatives.
