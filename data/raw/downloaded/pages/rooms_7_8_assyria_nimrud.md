# Assyria: Nimrud

The Neo-Assyrian king Ashurnasirpal II built the Northwest Palace at Nimrud. Its rooms were decorated with carved stone panels showing military campaigns, ritual scenes, protective beings and royal hunting.

The reliefs now displayed in Rooms 7-8 originally stood in the throne room and royal apartments of the palace.
